# Restricted Jurisdictions

EVPoly is unavailable in certain countries and regions due to regulatory, sanctions, or platform restrictions.

Use of EVPoly from a restricted jurisdiction is prohibited. Use of VPNs, proxies, relays, or similar services to bypass geographic restrictions is also prohibited.

## Countries

The current restricted-country list is:

1. United States
2. Iran
3. North Korea
4. Cuba
5. Russia
6. Belarus
7. Venezuela
8. Singapore
9. Thailand
10. Vietnam

## Regions

The current restricted-region list is:

- Ontario
- Crimea
- Donetsk
- Luhansk

## Notes

- EVPoly may deny access in these jurisdictions at app open and before sensitive actions such as onboarding or bot start.
- If location verification is temporarily unavailable, EVPoly may require an additional compliance acknowledgment before allowing use.
- This policy may be updated from time to time without prior notice.
